Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

1. Information We Collect

This bot may process Telegram user IDs, chat IDs, file identifiers, captions, and information needed to provide its file-sharing service.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used to deliver requested files, manage sharing links, and operate the bot.

3. File Storage

File identifiers and related information may be stored by the bot platform. Actual files are delivered through Telegram.

4. Automatic Deletion

Messages sent by the bot may be deleted after a configured period. This does not necessarily delete stored file information or sharing links.

5. Data Sharing and Security

We do not intentionally sell users' personal information. Telegram and the hosting platform may process data under their own policies. Absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

6. Data Retention

Stored information may remain until deleted by the administrator or platform.

7. Privacy Requests

Contact the bot administrator for privacy questions or requests concerning your data.

8. Policy Changes

This policy may be updated when the bot's features or data practices change.
